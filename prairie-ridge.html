The Design

The Prairie Ridge Residence was designed for a family of four who left Chicago in search of space, quiet, and a deeper connection to the land. Sited on a south-facing ridge with views across restored prairie, the home is organized around a central great room that opens on three sides to the landscape.

Cedar cladding, locally quarried limestone, and blackened steel accents give the home a material warmth that ages gracefully against the Wisconsin seasons. Deep roof overhangs provide summer shade while allowing low winter sun to warm the interior — a passive strategy well suited to the Midwest climate.

The interior is open and connected, with a kitchen and dining area that flow into the great room, a private wing for bedrooms, and a dedicated home office — a requirement for the family's hybrid work life. Every space was modeled in BIM and reviewed in 3D with the clients before construction began.
