The Studio

Founded in 2014, Meridian + Partners is a fully remote architecture practice focused exclusively on custom residential design. We work with homeowners across the country — no matter where you build, we meet you online.

Our process is collaborative and unhurried. We listen first, draw often, and build only what we believe in. Every project is led personally by a principal from first conversation to final drawings, with regular video calls and a shared online workspace keeping you involved at every step.

We design in BIM (Building Information Modeling), which means every project is modeled in three dimensions from day one — not just drawn in 2D. This allows us to coordinate structure, systems, and materials with precision, catch conflicts before construction begins, and produce highly accurate construction documents for your builder.

Throughout the design process, we share real-time 3D visualizations so you can walk through your home before it's built. From interactive models to photorealistic renderings, you'll see exactly how spaces feel, how light moves through the day, and how materials come together — all reviewed together online, no matter where you are.
