The Studio

Founded in 2014, Meridian Studio is a fully remote architecture practice focused exclusively on custom residential design. We work with homeowners across the country — no matter where you build, we meet you online.

Our process is collaborative and unhurried. We listen first, draw often, and build only what we believe in. Every project is led personally by a principal from first conversation to final drawings, with regular video calls and a shared online workspace keeping you involved at every step.

Services

Custom residential design, delivered entirely online.

New Custom Homes

Ground-up design for your site and your way of living — from feasibility and concept through full construction documents, all shared and reviewed online.

Renovations & Additions

Reimagining an existing home — layouts, additions, and interior architecture that honor what's there while making it work for how you live now.

Interior Architecture

Integrated interiors — millwork, material palettes, lighting, and finishes designed in concert with the architecture of your home.

Builder Coordination

We coordinate with your local builder remotely — reviewing bids, answering RFIs, and refining details through video calls and shared drawings.
